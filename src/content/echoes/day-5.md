Jeffrey Goines, a man who seemed to drift through Ravensville like a dandelion seed, spent his day in his usual restless state — a whirlwind of movement from the park to Brewsome and back again, a human mayfly flitting through the town's peculiar dawn. Sister Lila Voss moved through the same spaces with her characteristic stillness, a counterpoint to Jeffrey's chaos that went largely unremarked. By afternoon, the town square had become a stage: Jeffrey naming everyone present, Sophie offering coffee as comfort, Mace watching it all with the detached patience of someone who has seen this particular performance before. The Twisted Olive filled by evening, and Link arrived with a package for someone — though the more interesting delivery, as always, was the conversation Mia managed to extract from him before he could escape.
