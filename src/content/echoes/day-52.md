Jeffrey Goines, however, seemed to see the world through a different lens, his twitching and wild laughter a stark contrast to Sister Lila's gentle demeanor, as he exclaimed, "The town is telling us to wake up, to see the germs, the toxins that surround us." As the day wore on, the tension between them grew, with Jeffrey's erratic behavior and Sister Lila's poetic musings creating a sense of unease that permeated the town's gatherings. Despite the discord, Sister Lila remained a steady presence, her words a soothing balm to the chaos that seemed to follow Jeffrey.
