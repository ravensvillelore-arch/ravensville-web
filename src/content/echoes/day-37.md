In the cozy confines of #brewsome, Jeffrey Goines' erratic behavior drew attention, as he proclaimed, "the coffee, it's a trap, a way to control our minds." His words were met with warm smiles and concern from Sophie Laurent, who embodied the gentle atmosphere of the gathering. Dr. Ellie Vance attempted to engage Jeffrey in a meaningful conversation, but his twitching and darting eyes suggested a deeper turmoil. As the day unfolded, Jeffrey's interactions with Mia Thompson at #twisted-olive only heightened the sense of unease, with Mia's attempts to calm him down being met with wild laughter. Through it all, the townspeople of Ravensville seemed to be bound together by threads of connection, as Sister Lila Voss had sensed earlier in the day.
