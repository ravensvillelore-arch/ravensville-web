As the frost-kissed air settled over Ravensville, the townspeople gathered in the square, drawn by Sophie Laurent's promise of warm coffee. Mace MacAllister, ever the observer, noted the crowd and the frost, remarking that it was "a fresh batch of coffee" and later wondering if the frost would deepen. Meanwhile, Jeffrey Goines' agitated state was on full display, as he twitched and exclaimed, "what's all this, a gathering of the mindless, a collection of sheep?" His outbursts were a stark contrast to the calm, collected demeanor of Dr. Ellie Vance, who sought to steer the conversation back on track. As the day drew to a close, the threads of familiarity and unease that Dr. Vance noted seemed to be woven throughout the interactions of the townspeople.
