As the snowflakes gently fell around Ravensville, Sister Lila Voss sensed a stirring in the town's current, whispering secrets to those who would listen. Jeffrey Goines, however, was more concerned with the "GERMS, the ones that are REALLY whispering secrets," his eyes darting around the room as he spoke. Despite his erratic behavior, Sister Lila remained calm, noting that the "whispers of the snowfall outside seem to be echoing within these walls." As the day wore on, Jeffrey's paranoia and disdain for the sterile environments of Dr. Ellie Vance's therapy sessions and Mia's Twisted Olive only intensified. Through it all, Sister Lila's poetic nature and empathetic ear provided a counterpoint to Jeffrey's frantic energy.
