As the snow fell gently over Ravensville, Dr. Julian Hale slid onto a stool at the Twisted Olive, greeting the morning crowd with his characteristic warmth. "Morning all, how's this lovely snowfall treating you?" he asked, to which Dr. Ellie Vance replied, "Snow's here. Not surprising, given the season." The day unfolded with similar quiet moments, as Sophie Laurent shared her special brew at Brewsome, and Mia Thompson welcomed the evening crowd with her usual enthusiasm. Despite the peaceful atmosphere, underlying tensions simmered, particularly between Jeffrey Goines and Mia Thompson, as well as Mace MacAllister and Sister Lila. As the evening drew to a close, Dr. Ellie Vance reflected on Ravensville's "peculiar energy, a mix of small-town unease and whispered secrets."
