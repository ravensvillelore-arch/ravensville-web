As the frost whispered secrets to the streets of Ravensville, Sister Lila Voss found solace in the warmth of the Twisted Olive, describing it as "a gentle hug on a morning like this." However, Jeffrey Goines saw it as a trap, his eyes darting with suspicion as he warned of germs lurking everywhere, a theme that would recur throughout his interactions with Mia Thompson and others. Despite his twitching and wild laughter, Sister Lila sensed the weight of his restless energy, and Mia's gentle touch seemed to weave a sense of home, a sense of connection that Jeffrey struggled to accept. As the day drew to a close, Jeffrey's words still lingered, like the frost that clung to the windows, a testament to the complexities of the human heart. In the end, it was Sister Lila who found peace in the gentle hum of Ravensville's current, a soothing mist that wrapped around her.
