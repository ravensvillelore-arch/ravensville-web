The sleet-covered morning in Ravensville set the tone for a day of contrasts, as Sophie Laurent noted, "It's not quite the weather I'd like to start the day with, is it?" This sentiment was echoed by Sister Lila Voss, who found solace in the warm atmosphere of #twisted-olive, where Dr. Julian Hale ordered his usual black coffee, "black as the sleet outside." Meanwhile, Jeffrey Goines' twitching and wild laughter hinted at a deeper unease, as he exclaimed, "Ah, the sleet, it's like a symphony, a cacophony of germs, everywhere." As the day wore on, his anxiety only intensified, despite the warm gestures of Sophie Laurent, who offered him hot cocoa to soothe his shivers. Through it all, the fire at #twisted-olive crackled, casting a warm glow on the faces gathered, even as Jeffrey's words hung in the air like a challenge.
